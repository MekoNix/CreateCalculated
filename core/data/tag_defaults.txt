# Items that stand for a tag when any member of it would do.
# A tag takes the first item below that belongs to it, unless another member is really cheaper,
# for example left over from the scheme or taken from stock.
# item | English name | Russian name | tag whose other members are the same thing
# With a name the item is shown under it everywhere, its picture stays the game's one.
# Other members of the last column's tag are left out of the item search.
# Tags with members that really differ, such as iron or zinc nuggets, are not listed.
